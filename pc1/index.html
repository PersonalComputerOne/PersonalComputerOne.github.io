About Bleach

Series Overview

Bleach (ブリーチ, Burīchi) is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Tite Kubo. It follows the adventures of high school student Ichigo Kurosaki after he obtains the powers of a Soul Reaper from Rukia Kuchiki.

Soundtrack

The Bleach discography was composed by Shirō Sagisu, released in eight volumes and an anniversary box set — all through Sony Music Entertainment Japan.
